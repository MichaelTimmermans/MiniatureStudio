        Storage
        
–

        

        

          
          
          
          
        

        
Photos, stacks and videos go to a MiniatureStudio folder on the disk.
          ext4, exFAT, FAT32 and NTFS work; the disk is mounted again at every boot and when you replug it.
          A USB SSD is fastest and wears least.
